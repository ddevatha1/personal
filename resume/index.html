First Author · Mentor: Joe Xiao (PhD student) · Under review, Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience · medRxiv preprint, Aug. 2026 · Code · Research log

  • Engineered Sentence-BERT discourse features: adjacent-sentence cosine similarity, global coherence, semantic slope, topic drift, and embedding variance, plus visit-to-visit Δ features and temporal metadata.

  • Controlled feature-source ablation: clinical-only R² 0.72, SBERT-only 0.46, linguistic-only 0.12, clinical + SBERT 0.76; reported non-nested selection/tuning as an optimism limitation.
X-Step: Four-Site Plantar-Pressure Insole — ML Lead, 2nd of 5 Authors2025–Present

UC Santa Cruz AIEA Lab (Prof. Leilani Gilpin) and MIT CSAIL author affiliations · Under review, IEEE-EMBS IECBES 2026 · Paper summary

  • Implemented a 59-feature plantar-pressure representation from four FSR402 sensors sampled at 25 Hz over BLE from an ESP32 (per-site peak/mean/CV, pressure–time integral, cadence, stance ratio, anterior load share) feeding a 540-parameter logistic-regression head.
  • Designed a sparse-vs-dense sensing study on 149 recordings from 15 adults using a public 32-cell insole: four sites preserved waveform shape (median r 0.72–0.99) but not magnitude (peak-to-peak r 0.43–0.74); center of pressure tracked anteroposterior (r 0.81) but not mediolateral motion (r 0.01).
  • Quantified subject-identity leakage on a synthetic 24-subject cohort: macro-F1 0.885 subject-grouped vs. 0.931 ungrouped.
