Research

Paper 1 · Computational linguistics / clinical ML
Predicting Cognitive Function Using Transformer-Derived Speech Representations and Longitudinal Coherence Features
May 2025–Present

First Author·Mentor: Joe Xiao (PhD student)

medRxiv preprint (Aug. 2026)·Revision recommended by the editors, Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience — submitted August 04, 2026

Preprint: medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.04.26359730v1·Code: github.com/ddevatha1/dementia-mmse-prediction·Write-up: deeptanshudevatha.com/research/speech-cognition

Submission post: deeptanshudevatha.com/#modal-dementia-25·leakage fix: deeptanshudevatha.com/#modal-dementia-12·data-join bugs: deeptanshudevatha.com/#modal-dementia-14·ablation: deeptanshudevatha.com/#modal-dementia-24

Question

Can transcribed speech plus the clinical history already on file forecast a patient’s next-visit MMSE (0–30)? Framed as longitudinal, patient-level supervised regression; most prior speech work is single-visit classification.

Data

DementiaBank Pitt Corpus (controlled access): 126 patients (69 dementia, 57 control), 2–5 visits → 173 samples.

Features

Designed a 53-dimensional feature vector: 12 per-visit features — Sentence-BERT discourse coherence (adjacent-sentence similarity, global coherence, semantic slope, topic drift) plus spaCy lexical/syntactic measures (filler rate, 50-token MATTR, inter-sentence repetition, POS-based fragment ratio, named-entity rate) — with 8 visit-to-visit Δ features, 4 temporal features, 20 PCA components of 384-d SBERT embeddings, and 9 clinical/demographic covariates.

Model

LightGBM, chosen for small mixed-type tabular data: RobustScaler → correlation pruning (|r| > 0.8, clinical variables kept) → permutation-importance selection (top 15) → 3× Gaussian augmentation tagged by parent patient → Optuna TPE over 10 hyperparameters, 200 trials, maximizing mean patient-grouped R².

Validation

Diagnosed patient-identity leakage: folds split by visit, so a model could score by recognizing a person. Rebuilt evaluation as 5-fold GroupKFold on patient ID, locking augmented copies to their parent’s fold and scoring only out-of-fold predictions on original samples; scores fell once leaked identity was removed.

Debugging

Found why entry MMSE, the expected top predictor, contributed almost nothing: two silent data-join bugs (renamed column; string-vs-integer patient ID) imputed one value for every patient. Fixing them was the largest single gain.

Research (continued)

Results

R² 0.840 ± 0.015·RMSE 2.75·MAE 2.02 MMSE points·Pearson r 0.917. Entry MMSE, baseline diagnosis and Blessed score lead permutation importance.

Ablation

Controlled feature-source ablation: linguistic-only R² 0.118, SBERT-only 0.458, clinical-only 0.716, clinical + SBERT 0.759. Clinical history dominates; SBERT beats handcrafted features and adds complementary signal.

Integrity & limits

Audited the manuscript against the code, removing six unused features. Limits: non-nested selection and tuning (CV may be optimistic); one cohort, no external validation; over-prediction below MMSE 10.

Paper 2 · Biomedical engineering / wearable sensing
A Four-Site Plantar-Pressure Insole: Transducer Error Budget, Feature Equivariance, and Sparse-Versus-Dense Agreement in Overground Walking
Jul. 2026–Present

Accepted for Presentation · 2026 IEEE-EMBS Conference on Biomedical Engineering and Sciences (IECBES) · Kuala Lumpur · Dec. 9–11, 2026

2nd of 5 authors · ML lead·UC Santa Cruz AIEA Lab (Prof. Leilani Gilpin)

Paper page: deeptanshudevatha.com/research/x-step·Lab: aiea-lab.github.io

Question

Can four strategically placed sensing sites (MET1, MET2, MET5, heel; ESP32, 25 Hz) capture regional pressure trends comparable to a 32-sensor insole? I engineered the machine-learning component and designed the correlation study that tests it, working from the pressure measurements our team’s four-site system generates.

ML pipeline

Built the analysis pipeline for this question: the feature map over rolling 4 s windows (100 samples × 8 channels) to 59 features — per-site peak, mean, coefficient of variation and pressure–time integral; window-level cadence, stance ratio, anterior load share, aggregate peak and total PTI — and a 540-parameter logistic-regression framework that classifies biomechanical risk from them (≈0.23 ms per update).

Correlation study

Designed the sparse-vs-dense study against an independent 32-cell insole archive (149 recordings, 15 adults, 298 foot-takes), comparing the cell nearest each of the insole’s four sites with its anatomical cluster. Shape survived (median r 0.72–0.99), magnitude did not (peak-to-peak r 0.43–0.74); four-site center of pressure tracked anteroposterior (r 0.81) but not mediolateral motion (r 0.01) — a limit of the four-site geometry. Descriptive only.

Evaluation

Quantified subject-identity leakage on a synthetic 24-subject, 9-class cohort: macro-F1 0.885 subject-grouped vs 0.931 ungrouped (majority baseline 0.040) — a gap larger than the spread across classifiers.

App Store: apps.apple.com/app/shopai/id6797083809·Project page: deeptanshudevatha.com/projects/shopai·Architecture: deeptanshudevatha.com/#modal-shopai-architecture·Hackathon page: devpost.com/software/smartshop-gj4q59

Architecture

React Native/Expo client over a standalone Express/TypeScript backend on AWS EC2 behind Nginx; the client holds no search logic — every search, plan and warm-up check runs server-side through a four-layer, one-direction pipeline.

Adapters

Designed a retailer-adapter abstraction normalizing nine chains (seven with live data) into one schema via four integration strategies: OAuth2 client-credentials REST (Kroger, Harris Teeter), anonymous-session GraphQL (Aldi, Sprouts, Publix via Instacart), headless Playwright (Trader Joe’s), server-rendered HTML (Whole Foods). Target excluded (terms of service).

Search path

Query correction → concurrent fan-out racing a shared response-time budget → per-store food/relevance filtering and ranking → explicit ok / error / unavailable / pending status per store. A per-ZIP state machine (starting → warming → ready) pre-warms OAuth tokens, cookies and the browser session off the request path.

Planner

A deterministic optimizer exhaustively scores all 2⁷ − 1 = 127 non-empty store subsets, so “cheapest” is exact; cheapest, fastest and fewest-stops are single-field sorts; only “balanced” is weighted multi-criteria. Zero LLM calls in search or planning.
