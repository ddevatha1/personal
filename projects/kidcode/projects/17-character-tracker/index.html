Character Tracker

Loading a public-domain story and running one chapter at a time through nlp. Extracting PERSON and GPE entities and counting them with Counter.

Where it falls

Week 5, Named Entity Recognition, Day 24 of 40 in the 2026 curriculum (Natural-language processing).

What students learned

Extract and count PERSON/GPE entities from a story chapter by chapter.

Concepts

public-domain story; one chapter at a time through nlp; PERSON and GPE; Counter.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 17: Lab: Auto Mad Libs II. Asking the player for words of the right type, then rebuilding the story with token.text_with_ws so spacing and punctuation stay correct. Swapping finished stories with classmates.

Day 18: Dependency Parsing: Who Did What?. token.dep_, token.head, and token.children, and finding subjects and objects. Drawing sentence diagrams with displacy.render(). Practice: "Sentence Skeletons".

Day 19: The spaCy Pipeline. The pipeline as an assembly line: nlp.pipe_names and what each component adds. Processing many texts with nlp.pipe() and turning off unused components for speed.

Day 20: Checkpoint: spaCy Cheat Sheet. Review games covering Weeks 1 to 4, a checkpoint quiz, and a personal spaCy cheat sheet. Pair debugging with error messages students have not seen before.

Day 21: What Are Named Entities?. Real-world names and facts hiding in text. Using doc.ents, ent.text, ent.label_, and ent.start_char, and decoding PERSON, ORG, GPE, DATE, and MONEY with spacy.explain().

Day 22: Seeing Entities with displaCy. Highlighting entities with displacy.render(doc, style="ent") in a notebook or displacy.serve() in the browser, and saving results as HTML. Lab: "Entity Gallery" of student paragraphs.

Day 23: When the Model Is Wrong. Why models guess: ambiguous names like "Washington", "Apple", and "Jordan", names from many cultures, and text without capital letters. Hand-labeling 20 sentences for an "NER Report Card".

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 3 of 4 for 2026, “Named Entities & Responsible NLP” (Week 5 + Days 33–34). More on the assessments.
