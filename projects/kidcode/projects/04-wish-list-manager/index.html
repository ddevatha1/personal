Wish List Manager

A menu-driven program that combines while, input(), if/elif, and lists to add, remove, and show items. Design the menu on paper first, then test in pairs.

Where it falls

Week 5, Lists & Tuples, Day 25 of 40 in the 2024 curriculum (Python from zero).

What students learned

Design and build a menu-driven program around a list.

Concepts

while + input() + if/elif + lists; paper design; pair testing.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 21: Lists: Organizing Many Things. Creating lists with [], zero-based indexing, negative indexes, len(), and slicing with [start:end]. Comparing lists with strings. Practice: "Top 5 Ranker".

Day 22: List Methods & Mutability. Changing lists in place with .append(), .insert(), .remove(), .pop(), .sort(), and .reverse(). Membership tests with in and not in. Practice: "Playlist Editor".

Day 23: Looping Over Lists & Tuples. Using for item in my_list, building new lists in a loop, and finding totals, maximums, and minimums. Tuples as read-only lists, and unpacking like x, y = point.

Day 24: Words in a List. Turning text into lists with .split() and back into text with .join(). Sorting words and finding the longest word. Lab: "Sentence Scrambler" that shuffles a sentence.

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 3 of 4 for 2024, “Lists, Tuples & Dictionaries” (Weeks 5–6 (Days 21–30)). More on the assessments.

Where it goes next

Before this: Project 3, Guess the Number Deluxe. After this: Project 5, Trivia Quiz Show.
