Project 10 of 20 · Year 2 · 2025

Virtual Pet Care

Designing a pet on paper, then coding hunger and happiness meters, a daily menu of actions, and saving the pet to a JSON file. Classmates trade pets and keep them alive.

Where it falls

Week 4, Objects & Classes, Day 20 of 40 in the 2025 curriculum (Building bigger programs).

What students learned

Design a class on paper and implement a persistent virtual pet program.

Concepts

class design; hunger/happiness meters; menu of daily actions; saving to JSON.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 16: Everything Is an Object. Discovering that str, list, and dict are classes and that methods are functions belonging to objects. Exploring with type(), dir(), and help(). A class is a blueprint, like a cookie cutter.

Day 17: Your First Class. Writing a class with class, __init__, self, and attributes, then creating several instances. Practice: a Pet class with name, species, and energy.

Day 18: Methods & Readable Objects. Giving objects behaviors: methods like feed() and play() that change attributes, and methods that return values. Using __str__ so print(pet) is readable.

Day 19: Objects Working Together. Storing objects in lists, objects that hold other objects (a Team with Players), and class vs. instance attributes. Practice: "Zoo Manager".

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 4 of 5 for 2025, “Objects, Classes & Inheritance” (Weeks 4–5 (Days 16–25)). More on the assessments.
