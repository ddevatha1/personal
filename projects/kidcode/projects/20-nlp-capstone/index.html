Where it falls

Week 8, Capstone & Showcase, Day 36 of 40 in the 2026 curriculum (Natural-language processing).

What students learned

Build a capstone core: load a file, run the spaCy pipeline, organize code.

Concepts

file loading; spaCy pipeline; functions/classes; daily stand-ups.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 32: Lab: KidBot Brain II. Using entities to pull out places and dates so "What's the weather in Paris tomorrow?" gets a pretend forecast. Adding a friendly fallback reply and testing with classmates.

Day 33: How Models Learn. How spaCy's model learned from labeled examples, why training data shapes what a model gets right, and how mistakes and bias appear. Students annotate 20 sentences and compare with the model.

Day 34: Responsible NLP: Bias & Privacy. Fairness, privacy, and knowing when not to use NLP. Class roundtable: "Should a school use NLP to read student essays?" Writing a "Limitations" card for a personal project.

Day 35: Capstone Pitch & Plan. Choosing a project: "Story Explorer", "KidBot Pro", "Privacy Shield Pro", "Time Traveler's News Desk", or an approved original idea. A one-page plan with goals, test texts, and a feature checklist.

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 4 of 4 for 2026, “Rules, Custom Entities & Chatbots” (Weeks 6–8 (Days 26–40)). More on the assessments.
