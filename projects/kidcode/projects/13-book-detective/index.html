Book Detective

Planning a TextAnalyzer class that loads a book and reports total words, unique words, longest word, average word length, and top words. Sketching attributes and methods on paper.

Where it falls

Week 8, Capstone & Showcase, Day 36 of 40 in the 2025 curriculum (Building bigger programs).

What students learned

Plan a TextAnalyzer class for book analysis.

Concepts

class design on paper; attributes/methods; statistics: total words, unique words, longest word, average length, top words.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 29: pip & Third-Party Libraries. What PyPI is and how pip install works, and the difference between a module, package, and library. Installing rich for colorful text, reading a README, and fixing a failed install safely.

Day 30: Lab: Class Survey Dashboard. Installing matplotlib and drawing bar charts with plt.bar(), titles, labels, and plt.show(). Loading survey answers from a CSV file and charting the results. KidCode 2 Curriculum S

Day 31: Loading & Cleaning Text. Loading a public-domain book from Project Gutenberg as a text file. Cleaning it with .lower(), string.punctuation, and .split(), and finding unique words with sets.

Day 32: Counting Words with Counter. Using collections.Counter and .most_common() to rank words, and stop-word lists to skip "the" and "and". Practice: "Top 10 Words" for any text file.

Day 33: Lab: Word Frequency Chart. Charting a book's top words with matplotlib and comparing two books: which words define each story? Adding average word length and vocabulary size to the report.

Day 34: Comparing Texts with Sets. Set operations &, |, and - to find shared and unique words between two texts. Measuring lexical diversity (unique words divided by total words). Practice: "Author Fingerprint".

Day 35: Language Is Tricky: Bug Safari. Testing our code on tricky text: .split(".") breaks on "Dr. Smith" and "U.S.A.", and .split() leaves punctuation stuck to words. Logging every failure as a cliffhanger for Year 3.

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 5 of 5 for 2025, “Libraries, Modules & Text as Data” (Weeks 6–8 (Days 26–40)). More on the assessments.
