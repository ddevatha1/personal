Project 11 of 20 · Year 2 · 2025

Monster Battle Arena

A Monster parent class with name, hp, and attack, plus FireMonster and WaterMonster children. A turn-based battle loop with random damage, type advantages, and a saved "Hall of Fame".

Where it falls

Week 5, Inheritance & The Arena, Day 23–24 of 40 in the 2025 curriculum (Building bigger programs).

What students learned

Design a class hierarchy for a turn-based battle game.

Concepts

Monster parent class (name, hp, attack); FireMonster/WaterMonster children; battle loop.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 21: Inheritance: Parents & Children. Child classes that inherit from a parent class, super().__init__(), and overriding methods. Practice: an Animal parent with Dog and Cat children that each speak() differently.

Day 22: Polymorphism & isinstance(). Treating different objects the same way: looping through a mixed list of animals and calling the same method. Using isinstance() and planning class families on paper.

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 4 of 5 for 2025, “Objects, Classes & Inheritance” (Weeks 4–5 (Days 16–25)). More on the assessments.

Where it goes next

Before this: Project 10, Virtual Pet Care. After this: Project 12, Turtle Art Studio.
