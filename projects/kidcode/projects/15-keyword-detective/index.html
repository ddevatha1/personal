Project 15 of 20 · Year 3 · 2026

Keyword Detective

Tokenizing a chapter, dropping stop words and punctuation, lemmatizing, counting with Counter, and charting the top keywords. Comparing with Year 2's Book Detective on the same text.

Where it falls

Week 3, Sentences & Grammar, Day 12–13 of 40 in the 2026 curriculum (Natural-language processing).

What students learned

Extract and chart top keywords from a chapter using spaCy.

Concepts

tokenize a chapter; stop words/punctuation removal; lemmatize; Counter; charting; comparison with Year 2 Book Detective.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 11: Sentences & Spans. Splitting text with doc.sents and working with Span objects such as doc[2:5], including .text, .start, and .end. Comparing spaCy's results with Year 2's .split(".").

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 2 of 4 for 2026, “Sentences, Grammar & the Pipeline” (Weeks 3–4 (Days 11–20)). More on the assessments.

Where it goes next

Before this: Project 14, Tokenizer Showdown. After this: Project 16, Auto Mad Libs.
