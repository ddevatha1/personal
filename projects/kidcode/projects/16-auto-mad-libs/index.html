Auto Mad Libs

Revisiting Year 1's Mad Libs: spaCy finds the nouns, verbs, and adjectives in a story and blanks out a random sample. Planning the program with a flowchart first.

Where it falls

Week 4, Grammar Detectives, Day 16 of 40 in the 2026 curriculum (Natural-language processing).

What students learned

Plan an Auto Mad Libs tool that finds nouns/verbs/adjectives and blanks a random sample.

Concepts

POS-driven blanking; random sampling; flowchart planning.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 14: Parts of Speech. Nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs, and how spaCy labels them with token.pos_ and token.tag_. Decoding labels with spacy.explain(). Unplugged game: "Human Sentence".

Day 15: Filtering by Part of Speech. Using comprehensions to pull out NOUN, VERB, and ADJ tokens. Finding the most common verbs in a fairy tale and exploring where the model is tricked ("Time flies like an arrow").

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 2 of 4 for 2026, “Sentences, Grammar & the Pipeline” (Weeks 3–4 (Days 11–20)). More on the assessments.

Where it goes next

Before this: Project 15, Keyword Detective. After this: Project 17, Character Tracker.
