Guess the Number Deluxe

Combining while, if/elif/else, random.randint(), and counters into a game with hints, a guess counter, and a play-again option.

Where it falls

Week 4, Loops, Day 20 of 40 in the 2024 curriculum (Python from zero).

What students learned

Combine while, conditionals, random and counters into a complete game.

Concepts

while; if/elif/else; random.randint(); counters.

What it asks of students

To finish this project a student has to bring the ideas above together in one working program that runs from start to finish.

The lessons that lead into it

Day 16: while Loops: Repeat Until. Repeating code with while, loop conditions, and counters. Stopping runaway loops with Thonny's Stop button. Practice: "Countdown Rocket" and "Three Tries Password".

Day 17: for Loops & range(). Looping with for and range() using one, two, and three arguments. Choosing between for (known count) and while (unknown count). Practice: "Times Table Printer".

Day 18: Counting & Looping Through Text. The accumulator pattern for running totals and counters, and looping through every character of a string. Practice: "Vowel Counter", "Palindrome Checker", and "Average Calculator".

Day 19: break, continue & Nested Loops. Leaving a loop early with break, skipping a round with continue, and loops inside loops for grids and patterns. Practice: "Multiplication Grid" and "Star Triangle".

How it is assessed

The project sits inside pre/post assessment pair 2 of 4 for 2024, “Decisions, Debugging & Loops” (Weeks 3–4 (Days 11–20)). More on the assessments.

Where it goes next

Before this: Project 2, Mystic Oracle. After this: Project 4, Wish List Manager.
