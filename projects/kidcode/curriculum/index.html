KidCode · Curriculum

Three curricula, one progression

Year 1 · 2024

Python from zero

Output and variables, strings, decisions, loops, lists, dictionaries and functions, in eight weeks of five class-days, ending in a text-adventure capstone.

Forty class-days over eight weeks, with 6 projects: 1 Mad Libs Machine 2 Mystic Oracle 3 Guess the Number Deluxe 4 Wish List Manager 5 Trivia Quiz Show 6 Escape the Haunted School

Some projects come back
