KidCode: Three Years of Teaching Python

2024: Foundations

Year 1 curriculum40 class-days · 6 projects138 completed projects

KidCode began in June 2024 with 35 students. Year 1 is Python from zero: output and variables, strings as data, decisions, loops, lists, dictionaries and finally functions, in 40 class-days and six numbered projects. It ends with a text-adventure capstone that needs all of them at once.

The curriculum, week by week

Every year was designed as 40 class-days in eight weeks, with a project at the end of most weeks. This is the design as written in the Year 1 curriculum; I didn't keep a dated log of each class-day, so the project pages are placed by where they sit in this plan.
