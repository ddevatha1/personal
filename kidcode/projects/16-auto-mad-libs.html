Project 16: Auto Mad Libs

Curriculum Day 16–17 of 4087 students completed itDeeptanshu Devatha

Auto Mad Libs is Project 16, across Days 16 and 17 of Year 3, and it brings back the very first KidCode project. This time spaCy finds the nouns, verbs, adjectives or names in a story, the program asks the player for replacements, and the story is rebuilt with token.text_with_ws so the spacing and punctuation survive.

What I taught that week

From the Year 3 curriculum, Week 4: Grammar Detectives

Day 16: Lab: Auto Mad Libs I. Project 3. Revisiting Year 1's Mad Libs: spaCy finds the nouns, verbs, and adjectives in a story and blanks out a random sample. Planning the program with a flowchart first.

Day 17: Lab: Auto Mad Libs II. Asking the player for words of the right type, then rebuilding the story with token.text_with_ws so spacing and punctuation stay correct. Swapping finished stories with classmates.

Day 18: Dependency Parsing: Who Did What?. token.dep_, token.head, and token.children, and finding subjects and objects. Drawing sentence diagrams with displacy.render(). Practice: "Sentence Skeletons".

Day 19: The spaCy Pipeline. The pipeline as an assembly line: nlp.pipe_names and what each component adds. Processing many texts with nlp.pipe() and turning off unused components for speed.

Day 20: Checkpoint: spaCy Cheat Sheet. Review games covering Weeks 1 to 4, a checkpoint quiz, and a personal spaCy cheat sheet. Pair debugging with error messages students have not seen before.

What students built

87 students built Auto Mad Libs. Every one chooses blanks by part of speech (token.pos_) and rebuilds the story with text_with_ws. The median program is 46 lines, against 17 for the Year 1 Mad Libs, and the blanks are chosen by the program rather than written into it.

def find_blanks(doc):
    found = []
    for i, token in enumerate(doc):
        if token.pos_ in WANTED_TAGS and token.is_alpha:
            found.append(i)
        if len(found) == MAX_BLANKS:
            break
    return found


def ask_for_words(doc, blanks):
    answers = []
    for position, i in enumerate(blanks):
        pos = doc[i].pos_
        prompt = f"Give me a {single_label} for word {position + 1}: "
        answers.append(input(prompt))
    return answers


def rebuild(doc, blanks, answers):
    answer_map = dict(zip(blanks, answers))
    pieces = []
    for i, token in enumerate(doc):
        if i in answer_map:
            pieces.append(answer_map[i] + token.whitespace_)
        else:
            pieces.append(token.text_with_ws)
    return "".join(pieces)
